# Lhub-Capstone-Project-Lai-Ka-Hing
-
Objective:
The objective of this project is to analyse the dataset encompassing key metrics related to banking transactions and card usage to:
1. Evaluate Banking Infrastructure:
•	Assess the distribution and utilization of ATMs and POS terminals (on-site and off-site) across banks to understand accessibility and service coverage.
2. Monitor Card Usage Trends:
•	Analyse transaction volumes and monetary values associated with credit (CC) and debit cards (DC) at ATMs and POS terminals (on-line and off-line) to identify patterns and consumer preferences.
3. Assess Financial Stability Indicators:
•	Examine outstanding balances (CC and DC) over time to assess liquidity and credit risk management practices within financial institutions.
4. Inform Regulatory Policies:
•	Utilize statistical analysis and data-driven insights to formulate recommendations for regulatory frameworks aimed at enhancing consumer protection, market integrity, and financial stability.
5. Evaluate Financial Inclusion and Regional Disparities:
•	Examine the geographic and demographic distribution of banking infrastructure and card adoption to identify underserved segments or regional gaps, ensuring policies promote equitable financial access across all communities.
